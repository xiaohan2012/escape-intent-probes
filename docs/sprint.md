## The sprint

**Event.** AI Incident Response Sprint, organised by
[Apart Research](https://apartresearch.com) and CeSIA, 11–13 September 2026,
online and in person.
Page: <https://apartresearch.com/sprints/ai-incident-response-sprint-2026-09-11-to-2026-09-13>

**Framing.** Three days to turn evidence from the July 2026 OpenAI sandbox
breach into usable defensive tools: containment standards, detection systems,
regulatory instruments, forecasting questions, tabletop exercises.

**Deadline.** Sunday 13 September, 23:59 AoE (= Monday 14 September, 14:59
Helsinki time).

### Tracks

| # | Track | Framing question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Containment Standards | "What standard would have stopped this?" — verifiable by a third party without lab network access |
| 2 | Incident Reconstruction & Forecasting | What the incident does and does not prove; concrete checks and predictive causal explanations |
| 3 | Regulatory Response | Documentation for EU AI Office enforcement, using post-Aug-2 enforcement powers over model providers |
| 4 | Communication Strategy | Frameworks for incident communication effectiveness and warning-shot amplification |
| 5 | Open Track | Documented research gaps outside the four tracks |

All tracks compete against each other in judging.

### Deliverable

- PDF research report, 4–8 pages excluding references and appendices, on the
  official template linked from the Guidelines tab (the template mailed in the
  acceptance email is the wrong one).
- Must cover: what was built and how it was tested; key findings; limitations;
  next steps.
- **Required appendix:** Limitations & Dual-Use Considerations.
- An artifact — benchmark, harness, detector, dataset, protocol, regulatory
  instrument — in a linked repo or appendix.
- Optional: public repository, 3–5 minute video.
- Report must be the team's own writing; AI tools for checking and debugging
  only. Every factual claim needs a source.
- Resubmitting under an identical title overwrites the previous file, so an
  early version can be submitted Saturday night as insurance.

### Judging

Impact & Innovation / Execution / Presentation, each scored 1–5, plus the
track-specific criterion. For Containment that criterion is third-party
verifiability and adoptability by a lab.

### Prizes and follow-on

$1,000 / $500 / $300 / $100 / $100 — $2,000 total across all tracks. Non-cash:
fast-track invites to the Apart Fellowship (3–6 months of supported
development), mentor connections, publication support, and transmission of
applicable outputs to regulators with team credit. Judging feedback lands within
two weeks; outputs are published under open licences.
